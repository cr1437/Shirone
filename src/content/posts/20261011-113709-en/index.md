# Guidelines for minors to apply for Bilibili co-branded debit cards

# Guide for those under 18 years old (over 16 years old) to apply for a Bilibili co-branded debit card

> Applicable objects: minors aged 16 and above and under 18.  
> Currently, the more common choices are Bank of China’s Bilibili co-branded card and China Merchants Bank’s Bilibili co-branded card.

---

## 1. Bank of China · Bilibili Co-branded Debit Card

### Required materials

- Household register
- Personal ID card
- Guardian ID card
- A guardian must be present at the venue

### Process

1. Accompanied by a guardian, bring the above materials to a Bank of China branch.
2. Register at the branch and explain to the staff that you want to apply for a "Bilibili co-branded debit card".
3. If the staff does not know the card, they can directly say "card linked to Bilibili" to facilitate the other party to find it in the system.
4. Apply for a co-branded debit card according to the requirements of the outlet. The specific card collection and activation methods are subject to the actual guidelines of the outlet.

### Notes

- Minors usually cannot apply directly through online channels and need to go to offline counters.
- It is recommended to call the branch in advance to confirm whether the co-branded card is available to avoid a wasted trip.
- The implementation details of different branches may be different, and the requirements of the local Bank of China branch shall prevail.

---

## 2. China Merchants Bank · Bilibili Co-branded Debit Card

### Required materials

- Personal ID card
- My real name mobile phone number
- If the mobile phone card is not under your real name, you need to bring your household registration book (you can take photos, but you don’t need to bring the original)

The application at China Merchants Bank is relatively simple and does not require a guardian to accompany you.

### Process

#### Step 1: Apply online

1. Download the "China Merchants Bank" APP on your mobile phone.
2. After registering and logging in, search for "Bilibili Debit Card" in the APP.
3. Find the application entrance, fill in the information as prompted and submit the online application.

#### Step 2: Offline card collection and activation

1. After applying online, go to the China Merchants Bank branch you selected when applying.
2. Note: The card collection outlet must be consistent with the outlet selected when applying.
3. Bring your ID card to the branch.
4. If the mobile phone number is not my real name, prepare the household registration photo.
5. The staff will assist you with card collection and activation.

### Notes

- China Merchants Bank does not require a guardian to accompany you.
- The card collection outlet must be consistent with the outlet selected during online application.
- If your mobile phone number is not your real name, you can take a photo of your household registration book without bringing the original.
- The specific rights and redemption methods are subject to the activity page of China Merchants Bank APP.

---

## 3. General reminder

1. Debit cards issued by those under the age of 18 usually have limited functions. For example, online banking or some electronic channel functions may not be available. For details, please refer to the instructions of the bank outlet.
2. The implementation details may be different in different regions and different outlets. It is recommended to call the target outlet before departure.
3. Bilibili co-branded cards usually come with relevant rights and interests of Bilibili. The specific collection method, time limit and conditions are subject to the bank's activity page.
4. Please bring your identity documents and required materials when applying to avoid being unable to apply due to incomplete materials.
