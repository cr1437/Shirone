# Hiroi Kikuri

*Vocalist and bassist of SICK HACK. The drunken angel, the Happiness Spiral, the natural enemy of Bocchi's wallet.*

## ✦ Who is she

Hiroi Kikuri (廣井きくり / ひろいきくり) is a character from **BOCCHI THE ROCK!**, the manga by Aki Hamaji — bassist, vocalist and leader of the indie band **SICK HACK**, which plays psychedelic rock at the Shinjuku livehouse FOLT. Her image colour is purple, <span style="color:#8B5FBF;font-weight:700">#8B5FBF</span>.

Her real-life prototype is Margaret Hiroi, vocalist and bassist of the band 88kasyo Junrei.

![Hiroi Kikuri, anime design](/images/characters/kikuri-anime.webp "Anime design")

## ✦ Her look

Purple hair, purple eyes, a baby face with jagged teeth, always squinting with a smile. A dress with geta (sometimes just slippers), a braid, stud earrings, and a ring tattoo on the back of her hand — that is the "death hairstyle".

When drunk her eyes turn into swirls; sober, they are drawn normally.

## ✦ Her personality

She loves drinking and stays perpetually drunk to forget her worries about the future and the present, calling it the **Happiness Spiral**. But the moment something serious comes up, she snaps sober.

Easygoing and loud; her live style is chaotic: drinking on stage and spraying it, flipping the middle finger, forgetting the lyrics halfway through. Yet at heart she is earnest — reliable in anything music-related, and generous with advice as a senior to Bocchi. She is well aware she is "not the best role model".

Drinking is her personality switch: once sober, her temperament is exactly like Bocchi's — timid, bad with people, cautious, terrified of the future.

![The Happiness Spiral](/images/characters/kikuri-spiral.webp)

## ✦ Her story

In high school Kikuri was a loner holed up in a corner of the classroom, and she never touched a drop. She started drinking only to calm her nerves for a first gig.

She attended the same school as Shima Iwasaki without knowing her; in university she admired **Ijichi Seika** and joined the light music club — only for Seika's band to break up right as she arrived. She then invited Iwasaki to form **SICK HACK**. Too earnest and timid to hype a crowd at first (their early stage outfits were kimono), she got used to performing drunk from then on.

After that, she is simply the woman you know: turning up at STARRY in a permanent haze, guaranteeing that the front row of every Kessoku Band gig is occupied by a heavily boozy woman.

## ✦ Memes & trivia

- A professional debtor: although her gigs pay decently, drunk nights keep wrecking livehouse gear, so she is always broke — teaming up with Yamada Ryō as the **bassist duo who never repays Bocchi**. Her usual drink is "Onikoro" (prototype: Nihon'onsha's Onikoroshi, 180 ml, from Hyogo).
- She lives in a 52-year-old firetrap of an apartment with no bathtub, and rotates through the showers at Shima's, Eliza's, Seika's and Nijika's homes.
- The "STRONG -KYOMU-" she drinks in the Happiness Spiral illustration is a parody of Suntory's STRONG ZERO; it appears all over Bocchi's daydreams in episode 6.
- She is one of the very few people who can make Nijika look disgusted. But seeing sober Kikuri, Nijika overlaps her with Bocchi and cannot bear it.
- She holds a driver's licence, but drinking is off the table while driving — sober at the wheel, she is all flustered and wordless.
- Protagonist of the official spin-off manga, *Kikuri Hiroi's Deep Drinking Diary*.
- The anime dropped the ring tattoo on her hand (it survives in the OP); her manga dress carries higan-bana patterns that the anime recoloured plain.

## ✦ Gear & character song

- Main bass: **Yamaha TRB1004J, Natural** (which she named "Super Ultimate Shuten-dōji EX"); she plays it with a plectrum like a shamisen player.
- Street rig: **Roland Cube Street** and the **YAMAHA THR30II Wireless**.
- Her character song "Yūrei wa Watashi Dake" is psychedelic rock; her voice actress Ayaka Senpongi does not drink in real life and had to act drunk by feel alone.
