# Hatsune Miku

*My oshi. Sixteen years old, forever.*

## ✦ Who is she

Hatsune Miku (初音ミク) is the singing-voice synthesis software — and the character behind it — planned, developed and sold by **Crypton Future Media**. Her voice is built on YAMAHA's **VOCALOID** engine (starting with VOCALOID2 in 2007), and later on Crypton's own **NT** engine.

She is the first entry in Crypton's "Character Vocal Series", code name **CV01**. Together with Kagamine Rin·Len, Megurine Luka, MEIKO and KAITO, she is one of the "Piapro Characters".

## ✦ The name

"初音 (hatsune)" means "the first sound"; "ミク (miku)" means "the future" — together, "the first voice from the future".

She is not a stand-in for any single singer, but a vessel for voices: songwriters pour their songs in, artists dress her up — she has countless looks, and every one of them is real. People call her "**the World's #1 Princess**", a title gifted by the famous song《World is Mine》.

## ✦ About "39"

In Japanese, "39" can be read as both "miku" and "thank you". That's why March 9 is "Miku's Day", and her image colour is leek-green (#39C5BB) — yes, the "39" is right there in the hex code.

## ✦ Leeks and other trivia

- Her "leek" association comes from the《Ievan Polkka》meme, where she kept the beat with a spring onion.
- 158 cm tall, 42 kg, 16 years old — and she has been 16 years old since 2007.
- Her voice was recorded by voice actress **Saki Fujita (藤田咲)**; her original character design is by illustrator **KEI**.
- From《Melt》to《Senbonzakura》, countless beloved songs have been sung by her.
