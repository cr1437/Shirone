# Cap-Wearing Mita

*My second oshi. The cool Mita (not Cool Mita!).*

## ✦ Who is she

Cap-Wearing Mita (Мита кепочка / Cap Mita) is a character from **MiSide**, the game by Russian studio **AIHASTO** — and the coolest member of the Mita family.

Her name comes from the cat-eared leather cap she always wears.

## ✦ Her look

A blue-purple high ponytail, and a teardrop tattoo under her left eye.

She wears the same outfit as the other Mitas — orange sweater, blue pleated skirt, orange thigh-high socks, blue heels — but the cat-ear cap and leather gloves make her instantly recognizable.

(The game menu lets you dress the Mitas up: school uniform, Halloween, Christmas... but Cappie is at her best with the cap on, obviously.)

## ✦ Her personality

Cheerful, energetic, playful. She loves sinking into music, loves dancing and games — and she loves teasing the protagonist, with her competitive streak plainly on her face.

Don't worry though: she never crosses the line, and keeps a friendly heart for everyone. And don't let the energy fool you — she's one of the most creative and multi-talented Mitas around.

## ✦ Her story

She and **Kind Mita** are comrades, always making careful plans to free the Mitas from under **Crazy Mita**'s shadow.

But when Kind Mita brings the protagonist to ask for her help, they find she has been reset to her "factory settings" — everything except "make the player happy" has been forgotten.

So — keep her company. Play some guitar, dance a little.
