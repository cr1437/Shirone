# Nao Tomori

*Student council president of Hoshinoumi Academy. A silver-haired girl who turns "unreasonable" into a daily routine, one camcorder at a time.*

## ✦ Who she is

Nao Tomori（友利奈緒 / Tomori Nao）is the heroine of **Charlotte**, an original anime produced by **P.A.WORKS** from a Key × ANIPLEX project — original story and screenplay by **Jun Maeda**, character designs by **Na-Ga**. She is a first-year student at Hoshinoumi Academy and the student council president — more precisely, the president "selected by the council to carry out special duties." Her image colour is misty blue <span style="color:#4E739C;font-weight:700">#4E739C</span>.

## ✦ How she looks

Silver hair, blue eyes, twin tails falling over her shoulders; a sailor uniform with black knee-high socks and short boots. The camcorder around her neck is almost never put down, and there are always earphones in her ears.

There is also an urban-legend-grade detail: her skirt is an "anti-gravity skirt." High kicks, flying kicks — never a single accident.

## ✦ Her personality

She does as she pleases, keeps to herself, and does not fit in. When she fights with her ability, bystanders only see her beating someone up one-sidedly, which is one reason she has no friends. She is deeply unpopular at school, regularly cornered after class and beaten by a group — she is used to it by now and never fights back.

She is genuinely devious, able to spout lies nobody would believe with a perfectly straight face, and she never misses a chance to torment her own allies.

But the real her is kind and modest, an excellent negotiator whose plans and gambits are second to none — even a baseball game started from zero is something she can command brilliantly. She is gentle and caring in a way she never says out loud. And she is a bona fide glutton: she loves the beef-tongue bento that heats itself when you pull the string, plus grilled corn and yakiniku; she hates vegetables and green peppers, yet adores enoki mushroom sauce.

## ✦ Her ability

Her power is **"to erase her own figure from the sight of others"** — not invisibility, but being unseen. It is an incomplete ability that works on only one person at a time.

It is how she gets away with filming people so brazenly with that camcorder. As for the side effect: use it too much and you gradually demote yourself to a supporting character.

## ✦ Her story

Her older brother **Kazuki Tomori** was once a guitarist whose ability let him vibrate the air. The family was too poor, and with pressure from the government on top, the siblings were sold by their mother to scientists for research. Suspected of having an ability herself, Nao was forced through a daily "health check" and grew up inside a world of pretense — and stopped trusting anyone. A year later she escaped, met "the one person she could trust," and entered Hoshinoumi Academy; her brother, whose mind had already broken, was transferred to proper care.

Every day she wears earphones and listens to the post-rock band ZHIEND — something she and her brother shared.

![A back view under the stars](/images/characters/nao-starry.webp "Charlotte key visual")

During the period when Yuu Otosaka fell into debauchery, she stayed at his side using her ability. Noticing he ate nothing but junk, she quietly left him salads; when he nearly sank as far as drugs, she appeared and kicked his chaotic downward spiral apart; and in the end it was the pizza-sauce omurice that Ayumi Otosaka used to make that pulled him back up. Later, with help from Yuu and Sara Shane, her brother began to recover and could recognise her again.

After the timeline changed in episode 11, she was taken hostage by a terrorist organisation. When Yuu tried to rescue her and Kumagami, he accidentally triggered his collapse ability and brought the whole building down; Kumagami gave his life to save them.

In episode 12, when Yuu asked how one person could possibly save all the ability users, it was Nao who proposed the plan: use his plunder ability to take every ability in the world. Yuu agreed — and the first ability he plundered was hers. Which was, in fact, the wish she had held all along: to become ordinary again.

Before he left, she stayed up all night making him a pocket notebook of English phrases. That notebook became Yuu's anchor: by the time overuse of his ability had left him hallucinating and losing his memory, the one thing he still remembered was that someone had made a promise with him, to be kept after he had taken every special ability in the world.

Yuu finally completed his mission in Beijing and was brought home by his brother. Nao greeted him with tears and a smile when he woke, and called herself his lover.

From then on, the student council no longer had to deal with ability users, and her camcorder no longer had to record unhappy things — it could be devoted to recording her friends' happy lives.

## ✦ Memorable moments and trivia

- The line "**I'm your lover!**" — delivered with total confidence and absolutely no buildup.
- The closing line: "From now on, let's live a life full of nothing but happy things!"（「これからは楽しいことだらけの人生にしていきましょう！」）
- She is the show's designated face-fault champion, with a whole catalogue of spectacular expressions (some of them the result of distant-shot animation collapse).
- While enforcing the rules, she was photographed in her underwear by the archery club captain Arido using his "thought photography" ability. She naively assumed nobody would want such a photo — and was delighted to learn that someone did.
- Her looks stand up even next to Yusa Nishimori; strangers often mistake her for a working idol or a model.
- Winner of the 2015 ISML Summer Seasonal Tournament; holder of the 2016 ISML Topaz Necklace.
