# Iseri Nina

*The band vocalist who ran away from Kumamoto to Kawasaki.*

## ✦ Who is she

Iseri Nina (井芹仁菜 / いせりにな) is the protagonist of **GIRLS BAND CRY**, the original anime by Toei Animation — vocalist and lyricist of the band **Togenashi Togeari**. Her image colour is red, <span style="color:#D90E2C;font-weight:700">#D90E2C</span>.

## ✦ Her look

Brown hair, blue eyes, a short cut with low twin-tails and little side-braid "sideburns" — and she is tiny, just 152 cm tall. Black tights or white three-quarter socks, always.

(Two more official "moe points": a flat chest and landmine-type fashion. Moegirlpedia's words, not mine.)

## ✦ Her personality

Introverted, earnest, honest — and impossibly stubborn. Once she decides something is right, she will not back down; when something is wrong, she has to say it out loud. Her emotions are always at full volume: joy, fury, hurt, all on her face.

And it's that heavy, wholehearted sincerity that makes every line she sings land like a punch.

## ✦ Her story

It starts with bullying. In high school she stood up for a bullied classmate — and became the next target herself. Even the classmate she had helped turned on her. School and family told her to let it go; nobody stood on her side.

So she refused to go to school, dropped out at 17, and left Kumamoto alone for Kawasaki to study for university on her own. Her first day went terribly — wrong train, dead phone, a frightened neighbour — and there were weeks when she spoke to no one at all.

Then, at her lowest, she met **Kawaragi Momoka**, singing on the street. Moved by that voice, she ended up running through the rain with Momoka's guitar on her back — and her life turned a corner that night.

Later, with Momoka, drummer **Subaru**, keyboardist **Tomo** and bassist **RUPA**, she formed the band **Togenashi Togeari**. From street corners they kept moving — arguing, growing, fighting: Nina wanted university *and* the stage; her beloved old band Diamond Dust got a new vocalist… who happened to be someone from her past.

Anger, regret, and songs — she leaves them all on the stage. The rest of the story? Watch the show.

## ✦ About "Togenashi Togeari"

Fans in Chinese call them "刺刺". The five members: Nina (vocals/lyrics), Momoka (guitar), Subaru (drums), Tomo (keys), RUPA (bass).

Nina writes the lyrics — all that stubborn "say it out loud" lives in her words. And go listen to《空の箱》— you'll get it.
