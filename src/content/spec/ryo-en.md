# Yamada Ryō

*Bassist of Kessoku Band. The quiet senpai, and the natural enemy of wallets.*

## ✦ Who is she

Yamada Ryō (山田リョウ / やまだリョウ) is a character from **BOCCHI THE ROCK!**, the manga by Aki Hamaji and its adaptations — bassist and songwriter of the band **Kessoku Band**, who also sings backing parts during performances. She is Nijika Ijichi's best friend. Her image colour is blue, <span style="color:#2E6FD9;font-weight:700">#2E6FD9</span>.

![Yamada Ryō, anime official art w-40%](/images/characters/ryo-stand.webp "Anime official art")

## ✦ Her look

Blue hair, yellow eyes, a short cut with an asymmetric sidelock covering one eye, a hair clip, a teardrop mole, and stud earrings — finished with black tights. Textbook "cool beauty".

The official profile could not be blunter: "A cool and aloof second-year high schooler."

(For the record, her official moe-point list also includes "bad drinker", "good-for-nothing" and "deadbeat". Very Ryō.)

## ✦ Her personality

Her hobbies are far removed from the mortal world: she is **the weirdo who likes being called weird** — "once you throw away your individuality, you might as well be dead" is her whole philosophy.

Usually silent, but talks nonstop the moment rock comes up — and once she is drunk, even more nonstop, and cruder with it. She looks calm and unruffled, yet is surprisingly delicate inside; under too much pressure her image changes completely (that beaming smile she greets cafe customers with — yes, you).

She hates anything life-threatening. Roller coasters are out; in driving school she touched the rear wheel over the line once and never drove again.

![Kessoku Band compilation film design, Yamada Ryō w-50%](/images/characters/ryo-design-theatrical.webp "Theatrical compilation character design")

## ✦ Her story

Pampered by her parents since childhood, she took up rock at ten — music deliberately opposed to her parents' tastes — just to make them think she had gone delinquent so they would interfere less.

She used to be the bassist of another band. She loved their early, raw, honest lyrics; when the others started chasing popularity, she left — and the exit got messy. Just as she was tiring of bands altogether, **Nijika** invited her to form a new one, and that single offer rekindled her.

Then comes the ending you know: guitarist **Hitori Goto**, singer **Kita Ryo**, drummer **Ijichi Nijika**, and bassist Yamada Ryō — "Kessoku Band" is complete in Shimokitazawa.

After graduation she did not go on to university. An unemployed shut-in whose parents nagged her to move out, she left home before sunrise and returned after they slept — and eventually moved to Saitama.

## ✦ Bass-player credentials

- Her basses are a **Fender American Professional I Precision Bass** (Olympic White) and a **Fender Player Precision Bass** (Polar White), with a mint-green pickguard.
- Her rig runs **Ampeg SVT-CL** heads into **SVT-810E** cabs — that is the sound.
- Monitoring: Sony **MDR-M1ST**; phone: Sharp **AQUOS R6** (Snapdragon 888, which suits her aura not at all).
- She plays guitar too: she coached Kita before the Shuka High school festival, and even taught Bocchi-chan and Haa-chan one-off lessons at 500 yen a class.

## ✦ Memes & trivia

- Her family runs a hospital and owns a seaside villa — she is the band's "rich" member, yet all the money goes into instruments, so she eats wild grass and weeds and borrows money from Bocchi. Fans call it **Ryō the debtor**.
- The "ripe mango" box: in chapter 2 she hauled out a box labelled "ripe mango" for Bocchi to play her guitar inside — Bocchi's first ever "live-house capsule".
- She bought the multi-string bass Kita ordered by mistake, at the price of "living on grass from now on" — and later posted her wild-grass meals on social media to an audience of three thousand.
- Her grades were a disaster, she restarted from first-year middle school maths — and aced the exam. The cost: **she could no longer play bass**.
- In episode 11 she shoveled omurice into her mouth in a maid cafe with such abstraction that fans dubbed it "Ryō County Snacks".
- Tallest of the four; and despite all that, she has visible abs (Nijika: "and she is the most dissolute of us all").
