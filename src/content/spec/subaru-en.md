# Awa Subaru

*The drummer of Togenashi Togeari. A competitive young lady — and secretly a total gamer.*

## ✦ Who is she

Awa Subaru (安和すばる) is a character from **GIRLS BAND CRY**, the original anime by Toei Animation — drummer of the band **Togenashi Togeari**. Her image colour is green, <span style="color:#76BD53;font-weight:700">#76BD53</span>.

## ✦ Her look

Long black hair with a hairband, a cowlick, and the famous "air-intake" bangs — beauty that "wouldn't look out of place in a fashion magazine" (Moegirlpedia's words). She mostly just wears her school uniform.

## ✦ Her personality

A cheerful, socially gifted "sunny" type. She smooths over the band's quarrels with flexible, tactful handling — so smooth that Nina once called her a "liar".

Underneath, though, she is a fiercely competitive rich girl. And don't let the looks fool you: she's a hardcore gamer. She genuinely rages when she loses, argues with strangers online, and games late into the night — earning plenty of scolding from Momoka.

## ✦ Her story

Her grandmother is the famous entertainer "Awa Tendo (安和天童)", and the family always expected Subaru to become an actress — which she doesn't want. As a small act of rebellion, she secretly joined her high school's light-music club; later, when Momoka formed a band, she was invited in as the drummer.

Her relationship with Nina started at arm's length — until the day she left her phone at Momoka's place. Staying behind to talk, she shared her past and her goals, and the ice began to thaw — enough for her to start calling Nina 「NI~NA」.

When her grandmother visited unexpectedly and a short acting job came up, she wavered, even offering to quit the band. But on the day of the festival, she made her choice: **she turned down the audition her grandmother arranged and stayed with the band** — and on stage, she finally said her own name out loud: "I am Awa Subaru."

(One more thing: after Rupa kicked her door in, she threw the whole band out — "never come again!" — and yet later let Nina crash there anyway.)
